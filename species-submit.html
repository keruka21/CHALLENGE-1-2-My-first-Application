Species submission form

To register a species, it is necessary to Login / Signup .
Enter the common name.
Write the genus and species in binomial nomenclature.
If you are unsure, select “I do not know” rather than guessing.
Between 20 and 2,000 characters. Do not invent the conservation status if you are not aware of a reliable source.
JPG, PNG, or WEBP; maximum 5 MB. Use your own photographs or those for which you have permission to use.

This interface allows for reviewing the fields, but it does not submit or store proposals.

Before sending

  1. Check that the species is not already in the catalog.
  2. Check the spelling of the scientific name.
