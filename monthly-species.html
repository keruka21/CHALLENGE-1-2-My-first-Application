Species of the Month

Each month we spotlight a species native to Biscay. November 2026: the European Robin — the red-breasted singer of the Basque woodlands.

November 2026 · Featured

European Robin

Erithacus rubecula

The beloved songbird of the Basque landscape. Symbol of Euskara, guardian of the woodlands, and the most recognizable bird in the Basque Country. Its name, txantxangorri , means 'red singer' in Basque.
