Cookie policy

Cookie policy

Find out how cookies and similar technologies are used.

This page describes the use of storage technologies in the educational prototype Flora & Fauna of Biscay.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small files that a website can save in your browser. Similar technologies also exist, such as local storage (localStorage), which is not a cookie.

2. Storage used by this prototype

The language and visual theme selection is saved in localStorage to remember preferences between visits. The prototype does not store accounts, sightings or proposals. These preferences remain only in the browser and are not sent to a server.

3. Third-party services

Some pages embed YouTube players and a live camera from Sopela. When loading that content, their providers may process technical data or use cookies according to their own policies. The project does not control those external technologies.

Google Privacy · YouTube Information .

4. How to manage storage

You can delete cookies and site data from your browser settings. If you clear localStorage, the site will stop remembering the language and theme you chose.

5. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when new features or external services are added. Before a real publication, the site owner, the providers and the exact purposes of each technology must be identified.
