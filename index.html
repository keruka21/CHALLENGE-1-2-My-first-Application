A territory full of life

Our objective

A community that observes to conserve the natural environment

Bizkaia brings together coastal landscapes, estuaries, forests, mountains and areas humid areas that are home to extraordinary biological diversity. This platform connects that natural heritage with people who want to know him and take care of him.

We organize information by regions and habitats, we gather sheets of flora and fauna species and facilitate the record of sightings. Each contribution helps build a shared vision of the local biodiversity.
