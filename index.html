Our objective

A community that observes
to conserve the natural environment

Bizkaia brings together coastal landscapes, estuaries, forests, mountains and humid areas that are home to extraordinary biological diversity. This platform connects that natural heritage with people who want to know it and take care of it.

We organize information by regions and habitats, we gather files of flora and fauna species and facilitate the record of sightings. Each contribution helps build a shared vision of the local biodiversity.
