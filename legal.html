Legal notice

Website ownership, terms and legal information.

This notice identifies the nature and terms of use of the educational prototype Flora & Fauna of Biscay.

1. Ownership and purpose

Flora & Fauna of Biscay is an academic outreach project developed within Challenge 1.2 by students of DAMi 1st year at CIFP Tartanga LHII. Its purpose is to present a demo of a website that contains information on species, habitats, regions and natural spaces of Biscay.

2. Nature of the content

The site is a frontend prototype. Its forms, profiles and participation controls are demonstrative and do not imply that a real registration, authentication or persistent storage service exists. The information does not replace the records or official communications of the competent authorities.

3. Accuracy and updates

We strive to cite institutional and reference sources, but the information may change or contain errors. For decisions on conservation, regulations, access to spaces or species status, always consult the most recent official source.

4. Intellectual property and licenses

Texts, images, logos, audio and third-party resources belong to their respective owners. When a license or attribution is indicated, its conditions must be respected. The robin sound is credited to Lawrence Shove / British Library, Wikimedia Commons , under CC BY-SA 4.0 .

5. External links and services

Links to organizations, centers, platforms and external players are provided for informational purposes. The project does not control their content, availability, privacy policies or terms of use.

6. Responsible use

Users must use the information while respecting regulations and natural spaces. The site must not be used to capture wildlife, disturb nests, disclose sensitive locations or carry out activities that harm species and habitats.

7. Contact and review

The contact details published in the prototype are not real. This notice is an academic reference text and must be reviewed by the owning entity or competent legal advice before professional use.

8. Image credits

All images on this website are either sourced from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) or Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0) licenses, or were taken by the project administrators. This is a non-profit educational project.
